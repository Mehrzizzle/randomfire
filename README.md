# 🌱 Momentum — Daily Tasks & Habits Dashboard

A beautiful, self-contained dashboard for tracking your daily tasks and building
lasting habits. No accounts, no servers, no build step — everything runs and is
saved right in your browser.

**Live app:** https://mehrzizzle.github.io/randomfire/

## Features

- 📆 **Day & Week views** — a daily checklist plus a full Monday–Sunday planner
  grid where every task/day cell is checkable and can carry a custom note
  (e.g. Gym → Thursday: "Orange Theory")
- 🗓️ **Per-day scheduling** — each task can run on any subset of weekdays
- ⭕ **Segmented progress ring** — one segment per task closes as you complete it
- 🔥 **Automatic streaks** — every task tracks its own consecutive-day streak
- 🎉 **Completion celebration** — confetti burst when you finish today's tasks
- 🌳 **Growth Garden** — a plant that grows from a seed into a flourishing tree
  across 7 stages as your completions accumulate
- 🖐️ **Drag-and-drop reordering** — grab any task's handle to reorder, in both
  Day and Week views (works on touch too)
- 🌱 **Streaks tab** — a per-task tracker where each area of your life grows its
  own plant, with current/best streaks, totals, consistency %, and a 14-day strip
- 🤖 **AI Coach** — a built-in chat window with a personal Claude coach that
  sees your live stats (bring your own API key), plus a one-tap fallback into
  the Claude app for kickoffs, tough days, weekly reviews
- 📝 **Notes & scratchpad** — sticky notes at the bottom of the dashboard for
  reminders, journaling, or drafting a message before sending it to your coach
- ⚡ **Rise tab** — 18 real comeback stories, top-3 autobiographies, quotes,
  affirmations, videos, and books to refuel motivation
- 🌗 **Dark & light themes**, personalized greeting with a daily quote
- 📱 Responsive — works on desktop and mobile
- 🔒 Private — all data lives in your browser's localStorage; nothing is uploaded

## Running locally

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it.

## Deployment

The site is served by GitHub Pages directly from this branch
(deploy-from-branch mode). Every push publishes automatically.
